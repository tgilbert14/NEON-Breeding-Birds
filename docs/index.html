What the counts can—and cannot—say

Six-minute point counts record birds detected by sound or sight at sampled points. Birds per count is a detection index—not population size—and a non-detection is not proof of absence. Use effort-rarefied richness for site comparisons; climate patterns are space-for-time descriptions, not causes or forecasts.
